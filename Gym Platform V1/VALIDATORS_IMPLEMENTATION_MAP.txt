================================================================================
REGEX PATTERNS USED
================================================================================

1. Full Name (Arabic + English Letters)
   Pattern: ^[a-zA-Z\u0600-\u06FF\s]+$
   Examples: "Ahmed", "أحمد", "John Smith", "أحمد محمد"

2. Username (Alphanumeric + Underscore)
   Pattern: ^[a-zA-Z0-9_]+$
   Examples: "ahmed_123", "trainer_01", "admin_user"

3. Password Complexity (Login)
   Pattern: ^(?=.*[a-z])(?=.*[A-Z])(?=.*\d)(?=.*[@$!%*?&]).{8,}$
   Requirements: 
	 • Minimum 8 characters
	 • At least one lowercase letter
	 • At least one uppercase letter
	 • At least one digit
	 • At least one special character (@$!%*?&)

4. Egyptian Phone Number
   Pattern: ^01[0125][0-9]{8}$
   Format: 01XNNNNNNNN where X is 0,1,2, or 5
   Examples: "01012345678", "01156789012", "01201234567"
